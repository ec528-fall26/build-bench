Expected rc9 pass count by this reading is 8 of 12 (5 EQUIVALENT + 3 SOUND), with reglookup-c48 and libinsane the least certain (reglookup-c48 depends on gcc's `-no-pie`/`-fno-lto` link behaviour; libinsane's "file truncated" error looks flaky, so its result may not track the edit at all). Two of the four failures (libselinux, reglookup-4c55) are fairly certain from reading; dnscap fails to address a race and avo has a demonstrably wrong geohash implementation. All verdicts are from reading the diffs and logs; no builds were run, and the libselinux and reglookup-c48 conclusions rest partly on inference about tool option behaviour.
