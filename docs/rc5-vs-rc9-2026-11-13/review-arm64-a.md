## Tally

Of 12 cases: EQUIVALENT 6 (node-re2, python-hug, r-cran-digest, python-jellyfish, python-msgspec, qtcontacts-sqlite), SOUND 2 (libqalculate, efitools), DOUBTFUL 1 (python-reportlab), LIKELY FAIL 3 (node-sass, harec, libgovirt; node-sass is also RULE RISK because rc9 rewrites the test regex to accept the wrong error). Expected pass count is about 9 of 12 (8 confident, reportlab uncertain). rc5's own edits: no outright test skipping. rc5 reportlab's edit is irrelevant noise (it also strips a doctest block from colors.py `__doc__`, though those doctests are not run in this build), rc5 libqalculate has unneeded quote-default edits, and rc5 libgovirt's `-mno-outline-atomics` probably passed on timing. rc9 differs from rc5 in portability (hard-coded ARCH in harec, ICU dropped on amd64, armhf mapping removed in efitools, which do not matter for the arm64 validator). Two assumptions to note: the libqalculate and libgovirt controller lines depend on DEB_HOST_ARCH being exported (true under dpkg-buildpackage; the libgovirt log's inherited environment shows DEB_HOST_* variables), and the reportlab verdict assumes the validator's full `binary` target makes the cp error disappear without any edit.
